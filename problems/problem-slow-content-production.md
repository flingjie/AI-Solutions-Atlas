# Slow on-brand content and outreach production at scale

This problem comes from `project-jasper` [1] and `project-clay` [2].

## Who

Marketing teams and GTM teams at large companies have this problem. Jasper addresses enterprise marketing roles [1]. Clay addresses GTM teams and sales reps [2].

## Pain points

- Isolated AI use does not scale. Jasper states that "using AI for individual tasks only gets you so far" (official claim) [1].
- Campaign production is slow. Jasper claims that teams can "get campaigns to market faster" and cites a customer claim of "1 day vs. weeks" for a multi-platform campaign (official claim) [1].
- Manual research and follow-up limit selling time. Clay states that its product automates research, preparation, and follow-ups "to let reps spend time closing deals" (official claim) [2].
- CRM records become stale. Clay lists "keep CRM records accurate, complete, and continuously refreshed" as a use case (official claim) [2].
- Brand consistency is difficult at team scale. Jasper states that admins set brand rules once and every generation follows them (official claim) [1]. `(inferred)` Teams need this control because many roles produce content.

## Current approach

- Teams use general-purpose AI tools for individual tasks [1].
- GTM teams do account research, preparation, and follow-up manually (inferred) [2].
